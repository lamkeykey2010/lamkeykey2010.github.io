Small icons. A bigger puzzle.

Widget Shuffle uses the rectangle rules of Shikaku inside a fictional home screen. Every app starts as a single icon with a number. That number tells you how many squares its finished widget must fill.

  1. Drag between two opposite corners, or tap the two corners, to draw a rectangle.
  2. Include exactly one app icon. Match the rectangle's area to that app's number.
  3. Fill the entire screen without overlapping any widgets or leaving a gap.

An app marked 6 needs six squares. A 2 × 3 or 3 × 2 rectangle might fit. Check the icons around it before choosing a shape.

As an app grows, its widget reveals fictional content. Hover over a widget to reveal its central Shrink button, or tap the widget on a touch screen. Shrink returns that app to its original icon. Undo lets you reconsider a move, and hints can help when the screen feels crowded.

Make room for a harder screen

Start with a compact phone layout, then work towards larger tablet puzzles. Some later levels use a fixed landscape screen. Every level is available from the start, so you can try a harder board whenever you like.

Each board has one complete solution. Completion records favor fewer hints, then faster time, then fewer moves. Finish a screen to copy a colorful result with a link to that level and share it wherever you choose.
